Where this CTC goes

Basic is 50% of CTC. PF is 12% of basic from you, and 12% from the employer. Take-home is what reaches your bank account.

What is assumed. Slabs, the ₹75,000 standard deduction (₹50,000 in the old regime), the section 87A rebate with marginal relief, surcharge with marginal relief and 4% cess follow the FY 2026-27 rules for a resident individual below 60. Budget 2026 left the slabs unchanged.

Not included: professional tax, HRA and LTA exemptions, employer NPS, tax on PF interest, bonuses paid outside CTC, and any tax on employer contributions above ₹7.5 lakh a year other than PF. Treat the numbers as an estimate and check with your payroll team or a tax advisor before you decide anything.
